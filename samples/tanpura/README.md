# Recorded tanpura strings

The four CC0 single-string recordings from Freesound user luckylittleraven's "Tanpura C Sharp"
pack live here under the names Freesound gives them (the short names are also found). Measured:
Sa at C#3 (138 Hz), Pa G#2, Dha A#2, kharaj C#2, each within 4 cents of its note; 44.1 kHz 16-bit
stereo, 5 to 8 s long. The loader normalises their quiet level (about -24 dBFS peak) and trims the
~0.1 s of silence before each pluck.

| String | Freesound page | Downloaded name (or short name) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sa (C#) | https://freesound.org/people/luckylittleraven/sounds/416605/ | `416605__luckylittleraven__tanpura-note-c-sharp.wav` or `sa.wav` |
| Pa (G#) | https://freesound.org/people/luckylittleraven/sounds/416598/ | `416598__luckylittleraven__tanpura-note-g-sharp.wav` or `pa.wav` |
| Dha (A#) | https://freesound.org/people/luckylittleraven/sounds/416606/ | `416606__luckylittleraven__tanpura-note-a-sharp.wav` or `dha.wav` |
| kharaj (low C#) | https://freesound.org/people/luckylittleraven/sounds/416597/ | `416597__luckylittleraven__tanpura-note-low-c-sharp.wav` or `kharaj.wav` |

Then choose the voice "Recorded strings" (or "Recorded, octave below") on a tanpura. The app
measures each file's pitch when it loads and shifts the nearest string to every note it needs,
so any subset of the four works; with all four the shifts are smallest. Licences: `../LICENSES.md`.
